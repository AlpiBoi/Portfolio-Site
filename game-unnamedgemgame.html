A simple incremental clicker game, made with expandability as a main priority.

Current Features

  • Mine system: click to mine ores, which respawn over time.
  • Shop system: Use the coins you earn by mining to buy upgrades and mine more efficiently.
  • Abilities system: Spend perk points gained by leveling up to unlock powerfull abilities to help you mine.

Technical Features

  • This game is built for expansion. It makes use of Scriptable Objects to make adding new gems, upgrades, abilities or content easily.
  • An event relay ensures components are always enabled when searching for a reference

Upcoming Features

  • Exploration & Combat system: Explore to find events and monsters. Fight in a bullet hell arena for various rewards
  • Relics & Inventory system: Find relics which grant various buffs and abilities
  • Encyclopedia: Check out your discoverries in the game, such as gems,relics and monsters.
  • Options:audio, visual and other options to customize your experience
