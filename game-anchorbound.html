Anchorbound: Into the Mire

A group project with Croak Studios where I worked as a programmer. I mostly worked on the enemy systems.

Come see Croak Studios on Itch.io by clicking here!

Download Anchorbound here: https://croak-studios.itch.io/anchorbound-into-the-mire

Features

  • Unity 6.3
  • Component-based enemies
  • Wave-based progression with upgrades
  • Anchors which provide various buffs

My contribution to the project

Enemy system:

  • Enemy Base script: contains methods shared by all enemies, which all derive from this class
  • Skeleton Frog: Swarming melee attackers. They use a targeting system to circle around the player and overwhelm them
  • Rock Golem: Ranged caster: It hits the ground to send out a shockwave
  • Croc caiman: I only was in charge of this enemy's lobbing spear attack, which calculates an arc from the croc to the player

Skeleton targeting system

  • A ring of invisible empty slots will drag behind the player. Enemies will be assigned to each slots and navigate towards those insted of going straight to the player.
  • The skeleton frogs retreat after each attacks and are assigned a new target location, making them dance and swarm around the player.

Mudpits:

  • When an entity which can move, player or enemy, it applies a powerful slow effect to them. This effect is completly removed when the entity leaves the mudpit
